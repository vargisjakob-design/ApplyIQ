Why it loses for study abroad
Zoho CRM
Zoho CRM is genuinely powerful — for a salesperson selling SaaS or manufacturing goods. But you're not selling products. You're guiding students through a 6–18 month journey with intake calendars, visa timelines, and document collection. At 10 counsellors on Zoho Standard, you're already at ₹1.56L/year before WhatsApp, add-ons, or a Zoho partner.
Why it loses for study abroad
Odoo
Odoo is an ERP — designed for businesses managing inventory, manufacturing, and HR simultaneously. Running Odoo for a consultancy is like hiring a construction crane to move a piece of furniture. Implementation alone can cost ₹50,000–₹3,00,000. And you still won't have intake alerts or a counsellor comparison report without custom development.
Why it loses for study abroad
HubSpot
HubSpot's free CRM is genuinely great — for basic contact management. But the moment you need reports or team management, you're on Starter at minimum and rapidly climbing to Professional (₹1,20,000+/year). HubSpot has no concept of study abroad pipelines, intake windows, or counsellor accountability reports.
